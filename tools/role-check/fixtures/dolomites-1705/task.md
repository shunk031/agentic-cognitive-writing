Task objective:
Write a stakeholder requirements specification document (StRS)

Task procedure/context:
We need to define the requirements for a system that can provide the services needed by users and other stakeholders. We will identify the stakeholders involved and their needs and expectations.

Input specification:
* Description: 2 paragraphs, what the product should do and how it should behave. * Intended Audience: 1 paragraph, who the product is for and what it is used for. * Stakeholders: 1 paragraph, who the stakeholders are, how important they are and how they will take part in the development of the product. * Business requirements: 2 paragraphs, all the functions it needs to perform with business in scope. * Specific requests: 2 paragraphs, specific functions the software should do and specific cases it should be able to handle.

Supplied input:
**Input Sections**

**Description:**

This software system will provide a platform for users to create, manage, and share their own deep learning models. The system will offer a user-friendly interface that enables users to easily upload and train their models. It will also provide a marketplace where users can browse and download models created by other users.

The system will be designed to be scalable and efficient, allowing users to train and deploy large models quickly and effectively. It will also offer a variety of tools and features to help users improve the accuracy and performance of their models, such as hyperparameter tuning, data augmentation, and model evaluation metrics.

**Intended Audience:**

The product is intended for data scientists, machine learning engineers, and researchers who want to develop, collaborate on, and deploy deep learning models, as well as students who are taking machine learning and neural network courses. It is also suitable for organizations that need a platform to manage and share their machine learning assets.

**Stakeholders:**

* **Data scientists and machine learning engineers:** Primary users who will create, train, and deploy models.
* **Researchers:** Users who will use the platform to experiment with new algorithms and architectures.
* **Organizations:** Users who need a central repository for their machine learning models.
* **Development team:** Responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining the system.
* **Product manager:** Responsible for defining the product vision and roadmap.
* **Students:** Users who will use the platform to collaborate for team projects, or study how the models work.

**Business requirements:**

* Enable users to easily create and manage their own deep learning models.
* Provide a marketplace for users to share and discover models.
* Ensure the scalability and efficiency of the platform for training and deploying large models.
* Offer tools and features to improve the accuracy and performance of models.
* Offer cloud storage so that progress is saved and users don't need to train the models again.
* Provide tools for users to work on models simultaneously.

**Specific requests:**

* Support for a variety of deep learning frameworks, such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Keras.
* Integration with cloud storage providers for data and model storage.
* Version control for models to track changes and collaborate effectively.
* Model evaluation metrics to assess the performance of trained models.

Output requirements:

* Purpose: 2-3 sentences, describes the purpose of the product. * Intended Audience and Use: 2-3 sentences, describes who the product is made for. * Business Purpose and Scope: 2 paragraphs, how the product will contribute to meeting business objectives, and what are objectives and goals. * Stakeholders: 2-3 sentences, who the stakeholders are and how they will influence the development of the product. * System purpose and overview: 2 paragraphs, why is the product being developed, what will it include and what are the most significant parts of it. * Stakeholder requirements: 2 paragraphs, business requirements, critical activities the product needs to perform, what the product must do, how the product should respond. * Functional and Nonfunctional requirements: 2 paragraphs, performance/safety/security/usability/scalability requirements.

It is written in a language which is familiar to the people in the organization. It is important to understand the stakeholders are not just the people paying for the system. It includes all stakeholders that interact with the system throughout its lifecycle.

Use only the supplied task context and input; do not retrieve or rely on outside sources.
