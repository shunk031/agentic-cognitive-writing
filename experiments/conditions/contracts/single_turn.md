## Single-turn contract

This is a single-turn task with no interactive user; never ask clarification questions. When the assignment underspecifies audience, purpose, or scope, apply genre-appropriate defaults and record the assumptions. Conditions with a `.writing/` workspace record assumptions in `.writing/assumptions.md`. Do not state assumptions briefly at the top of the deliverable. Conditions without `.writing/` apply assumptions without an extra preamble. Do not change the deliverable's content contract.
