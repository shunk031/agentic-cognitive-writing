## Single-turn contract

This is a single-turn task with no interactive user; never ask clarification questions. When the assignment underspecifies audience, purpose, or scope, apply genre-appropriate defaults. The `agentic-cog-writer` skill and its `cognitive-writing-*` variants record these assumptions in the restatement in `.writing/assignment.md`; every other skill applies them without recording them and creates no file for them. Do not state assumptions at the top of the deliverable. Do not change the deliverable's content contract. Never include a skill acknowledgment line, such as `I read <skill name>.`, in the deliverable or the final response.
