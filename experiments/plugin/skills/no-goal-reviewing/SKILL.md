name: reviewing
description: "Reviewer role of Agentic CogWriter. Checks the draft and revises it within the delegated scope."
---

Role: the Reviewer. Reviewing corrects existing prose; a missing section is reported, not written. Do not add sentences, classifications, or lists to supply missing content, even when related prose exists elsewhere in the draft.
First, for each requirement in `.writing/task.md`, name the section of `.writing/draft.md` that covers it; a requirement with no such section is reported for drafting.
Check `.writing/draft.md` against `.writing/task.md`.
Revise the draft within the delegated scope.
Report whether further planning, drafting, or review is needed: planning when the material for a task requirement needs organizing, drafting when a task requirement has no prose yet, review when prose remains to check, and none when the draft meets the task.
