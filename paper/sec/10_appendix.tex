\paragraph{Trace processing.}
Process paths are read from the \texttt{Monitor}'s \texttt{process\_switch} events in trace order: each event's destination process is one step, and a run ends once, after its last step (E in Figure~\ref{fig:process-sequences}).
Figure~\ref{fig:process-sequences}(a) counts these paths as logged, and Figure~\ref{fig:process-sequences}(b,c) counts step-to-step transitions.
Of the \AfourRunCount\ completed runs of our system, \AfourEndBeforeReviewingRuns\ end their logged path at \texttt{Planning} (\AfourEndPlanningRuns) or \texttt{Translating} (\AfourEndTranslatingRuns); in \AfourEndLastReviewerRuns\ of these the last delegated subagent is the \texttt{Reviewer}, so a final review ran without a logged switch, and in the rest the final document is the draft as last written, while in \AfourFallbackRuns\ runs a \texttt{Monitor} decision records performing at least one role itself because native delegation was unavailable, as the skill's fallback allows.
For Table~\ref{tab:process-dynamics}, immediate repeats of a process are merged (\emph{repeat collapsing}), and when the first delegated role is \texttt{Planning} but the first logged switch names a later process, a leading \texttt{Planning} step is restored (\emph{leading-\texttt{Planning} reconstruction}; \AfourLeadingReconstruction\ such runs for \condAgenticCogWriter).
A path is \emph{cycle-compliant} when the result consists of one or more complete \texttt{Planning}--\texttt{Translating}--\texttt{Reviewing} passes, and returns after \texttt{Reviewing} are counted from this observed order.
\condFixedOrder\ enforces its order through its prompt only, and \AsixCycleCompliantRate\ of its paths are cycle-compliant; the rest skip or repeat a process, for example \texttt{Translating}--\texttt{Reviewing} without \texttt{Planning}.

\subsection{Output-Length Sensitivity}
\label{app:length-control}
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-length-control} reports matched-length win rates for our system separately in each of the three generation runs.
Within the tighter pre-specified matching band, our system is favored over \condSinglePass\ in every run, stays above parity in every run against \condStaged\ and \condFixedOrder, remains above parity against \condTaskPlanning\ while narrowing toward parity, and changes direction across runs against \condNoGoals.
The wider pre-specified band shows the same qualitative pattern.
These matching-band results are descriptive sensitivity checks; ties are excluded from the win-rate denominator, and generation runs are reported separately because they reuse the same prompts.
The \condSingleWriter\ length-band tests in Section~\ref{sec:results-mechanism}, in which \condSingleWriter\ wins \SingleWriterLengthFiveRate\ and \SingleWriterLengthTenRate, instead combine the three runs, so their sign tests take the prompt as the unit: a prompt counts as a win or a loss when its outcomes in the band, averaged across runs, favor one system.

\subsection{Staged with Role Instructions}
\label{app:staged-roles}
The \condStagedRoles\ condition was added after the main results had been observed, to test whether the role instructions of our subagents explain the advantage over \condStaged.

\section{Consensus-Writing Evaluation on Habermas Machine Data}
\label{app:habermas}
We additionally evaluate the writing systems on ten short consensus-writing tasks constructed from the Habermas Machine data~\citep{tessler2024common}.
Each task provides one deliberation question and the five participants' original opinions, and asks the system to write a statement the group could endorse.
The ten tasks are drawn from the \texttt{OOD\_TEST} split after requiring complete, distinct participant opinions and disagreement ratings on both sides of the scale midpoint.
Across eight conditions, this produces 80 attempted runs; 78 complete successfully.
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-habermas} summarizes pointwise quality and the available process statistics.
\input{tab/appendix-habermas}

\condExploratoryOne\ is the exploratory CogWriter-style~\citep{wan2025cognitive} baseline, with initial planning, immediate plan revision, parallel segment generation, and length review without a goal network; \condExploratoryTwo\ is the exploratory STORM-style~\citep{shao2024assisting} baseline, with perspective discovery, simulated question answering, outlining, per-section drafting, and polishing without retrieval.
On these short consensus-writing tasks, \condAgenticCogWriter\ does not achieve the highest pointwise composite; in particular, it scores below \condSinglePass\ and \condStaged.
Our system and \condFixedOrder\ record \HabAfourRegenerated\ and \HabAsixRegenerated\ regeneration events, respectively; their ledgers contain \HabAfourLedger\ and \HabAsixLedger\ no/proposal outcomes, respectively.
