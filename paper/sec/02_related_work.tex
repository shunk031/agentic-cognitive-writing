\paragraph{Language agents and document production.}
Language-agent research studies architectures that interleave reasoning with actions, tools, memory, and feedback~\citep{yao2023react,shinn2023reflexion,sumers2024cognitive}, while writing-support systems assist planning, drafting, and revision~\citep{gero2022design}. Agentic research systems increasingly connect these capabilities to report or paper generation after literature review and experimentation~\citep{schmidgall2025agentlab,lu2024aiscientist}. Our setting focuses on the internal organization of that writing stage when a general-purpose agent is responsible for producing the document.

\paragraph{Long-form generation and planning.}
Long-form generation becomes harder to control and evaluate as outputs grow in length~\citep{tan2024proxyqa,wu2026superwriter}, motivating explicit planning and decomposition~\citep{yao2019plan,yang2022re3,shao2024assisting,bai2025longwriter,wan2025cognitive,xiong2025beyond,sun2026cot}. Plan-and-Write~\citep{yao2019plan}, Re$^3$~\citep{yang2022re3}, STORM~\citep{shao2024assisting}, and LongWriter~\citep{bai2025longwriter} organize generation around plans, revisions, sections, or manageable content units; CogWriter~\citep{wan2025cognitive}, WriteHERE~\citep{xiong2025beyond}, IS-CoT~\citep{sun2026cot}, and SuperWriter~\citep{wu2026superwriter} make that structure or refinement increasingly adaptive. The central distinction in our work is the unit selected during execution: these systems primarily advance content, plan, task, or refinement structure, whereas \condAgenticCogWriter\ selects among writing processes.

\paragraph{Iteration and cognitive models of writing.}
Computational work has studied iterative writing through repeated model feedback and through models of human revision~\citep{madaan2023self,du2022iterative}. Cognitive writing research offers a complementary process decomposition: \citet{flower1981cognitive} distinguish \texttt{Planning}, \texttt{Translating}, and \texttt{Reviewing}, and \citet{gero2022design} use these processes to organize writing-support systems; CogWriter~\citep{wan2025cognitive} likewise draws on cognitive writing theory to structure planning, generation, and revision. \condAgenticCogWriter\ instead makes the writing process itself the online action selected by the agent and tests that choice through matched alternatives, ablations, and traces.
