\paragraph{Pairwise preference.}

The directional pattern is broader than the corrected evidence: \condFull\ remains above \condSinglePass\ and \condTaskPlanning\ in all nine cells, while \condFull\ versus \condStaged\ keeps the DoLoMiTes exception in the third replication.

\subsection{RQ2: What carries the effect?}
\label{sec:results-mechanism}

Table~\ref{tab:pairwise-results} reports the four ablation rows; RQ2 yields bounded comparisons rather than equality tests. Under same-family judging across three replications, \condFull\ versus \condNoGoals\ is a bounded null: the pooled win rate is \PooledFourFiveRateMean\ (SD \PooledFourFiveRateSD) for \condFull. \condFull\ versus \condFixedOrder\ has no per-benchmark cell surviving Holm correction in any replication. The untested pairs are \condNoGoals\ and \condFixedOrder\ with \condSinglePass, \condStaged, or \condTaskPlanning, and \condSingleWriter\ with \condStaged, \condTaskPlanning, or \condFixedOrder. The intervals do not establish equality or exclude smaller effects.

Table~\ref{tab:pairwise-results} shows the remaining ablation pattern: \condSingleWriter\ versus \condSinglePass\ is mixed across benchmarks, with \condSingleWriter\ winning on WritingBench, losing on HelloBench, and mixed on DoLoMiTes across all three replications. No HelloBench cell survives Holm correction after collapse. \condSingleWriter\ wins only \PooledSevenFourRateMean\ (SD \PooledSevenFourRateSD) of non-tied comparisons against \condFull\ and \PooledSevenFiveRateMean\ (SD \PooledSevenFiveRateSD) against \condNoGoals\ in the pooled summaries. These contrasts are consistent with delegated process roles carrying the effect, but they do not isolate that interpretation because \condSingleWriter\ changes recursion granularity and output length and \condTaskPlanning\ changes the delegated unit.

\subsection{RQ3: Process dynamics}
\label{sec:results-dynamics}

Table~\ref{tab:process-summary} shows what the delegating architecture costs and how much it delegates in the first replication: \condFull\ uses \AfourOutputTokens\ output-plus-reasoning tokens per attempted run and \AfourMeanSeconds\ seconds per completed run against \AoneOutputTokens\ tokens and \AoneMeanSeconds\ seconds for \condSinglePass, and it spawns \AfourSpawns\ delegated invocations per attempted run against \AthreeSpawns\ for \condTaskPlanning, which uses more tokens and loses the pairwise contrast. The table also reports completion, median output units, and input tokens for all seven conditions.

The process-trace analysis reports means across the three replications, with sample standard deviations (SD) in parentheses. A ledger entry is the enumeration recorded when \texttt{Reviewing} hands control back, listing every regeneration verdict or stating that none was proposed. Across the three replications, the \condFull\ traces contain \AfourGoalCreatedMean\ (SD \AfourGoalCreatedSD) goal-creation events and \AfourGoalDevelopedMean\ (SD \AfourGoalDevelopedSD) goal-development events per run. Its post-\texttt{Reviewing} ledger contains \AfourLedgerEntriesMean\ (SD \AfourLedgerEntriesSD) entries per run, including \AfourLedgerWithProposalMean\ (SD \AfourLedgerWithProposalSD) explicit regeneration proposals; accepted regeneration occurs between \AfourGoalRegeneratedRunOne\ and \AfourGoalRegeneratedRunThree\ times across the three replications.
