\subsection{Observed Writing-Process Behavior}

Table~\ref{tab:process-dynamics} shows a mostly simple writing loop after repeat collapsing and leading-\texttt{Planning} reconstruction: \AfourCycleCompliantRate\ of complete paths conform to the \texttt{Planning}--\texttt{Translating}--\texttt{Reviewing} cycle structure, while \AfourReviewingToTranslatingRate\ of runs return from \texttt{Reviewing} to \texttt{Translating}.

The lower panel of Table~\ref{tab:process-dynamics} shows similar win rates against \condFixedOrder\ and \condSingleWriter\ for one-cycle runs and runs with other paths, so departures yield no larger quality advantage in this observational split.\footnote{See Appendix~\ref{app:process-behavior} for process, goal, and resource statistics.}
