\subsection{Direct Comparison of Writing Systems}
\label{sec:results-effectiveness}

Table~\ref{tab:pairwise-results} reports a system-level separation in direct same-prompt comparisons.
\condAgenticCogWriter\ is preferred to \condSinglePass, \condStaged, and \condTaskPlanning, with win rates of \PooledFourOneRateMean, \PooledFourTwoRateMean, and \PooledFourThreeRateMean, respectively, when order-consistent wins and losses are summed over benchmarks in each generation run and averaged over runs.
The direction holds in every benchmark--run test against \condSinglePass\ and \condTaskPlanning\ and in all but one against \condStaged.

Under the pre-specified within-benchmark Holm correction, \PooledFourOneHolmCellsTotal, \PooledFourThreeHolmCellsTotal, and \PooledFourTwoHolmCellsTotal\ of the nine benchmark--run tests separate our system from \condSinglePass, \condTaskPlanning, and \condStaged, respectively.\footnote{See Appendix~\ref{app:pairwise-detail} for run-level intervals and corrected results.}
The preference also persists under more conservative accounting: counting every attempted prompt yields win and loss shares of \UncondFullSinglePassWinShare\ and \UncondFullSinglePassLossShare\ (\condSinglePass), \UncondFullStagedWinShare\ and \UncondFullStagedLossShare\ (\condStaged), and \UncondFullTaskPlanningWinShare\ and \UncondFullTaskPlanningLossShare\ (\condTaskPlanning), and assigning every missing pair against our system still leaves win rates of at least \WorstCaseFullSinglePassRate\ (\condSinglePass), \WorstCaseFullStagedRate\ (\condStaged), and \WorstCaseFullTaskPlanningRate\ (\condTaskPlanning).

\subsection{Observed Writing-Process Behavior}
\label{sec:results-process-dynamics}

Table~\ref{tab:process-dynamics} shows a mostly simple writing loop after repeat collapsing and leading-\texttt{Planning} reconstruction: \AfourCycleCompliantRate\ of complete paths conform to the \texttt{Planning}--\texttt{Translating}--\texttt{Reviewing} cycle structure, while \AfourReviewingToTranslatingRate\ of runs return from \texttt{Reviewing} to \texttt{Translating}.
Full replanning (\AfourReviewingToPlanningRate) and goal replacement (\AfourRegenerationRate) are rare, so adaptivity is used mainly for targeted redrafting.
Figure~\ref{fig:process-sequences} shows the same concentration: the forward \texttt{Planning}--\texttt{Translating}--\texttt{Reviewing} progression is most frequent, so our system looks close to \condFixedOrder.
Table~\ref{tab:trace-outcome} shows similar pairwise win rates against \condFixedOrder\ and \condSingleWriter\ for runs with one \texttt{Planning}--\texttt{Translating}--\texttt{Reviewing} cycle and runs with other paths, so observed departures do not yield a larger quality advantage in this observational split.\footnote{See Appendix~\ref{app:process-behavior} for process, goal, and resource statistics.}
