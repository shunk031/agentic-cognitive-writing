\subsection{Ablations (RQ2)}
\label{sec:results-mechanism}

The two confirmatory single-factor interventions both leave the pairwise result near even, and no benchmark-by-replication cell survives Holm correction. Thus, neither removing the explicit goal representation nor replacing adaptive process choice with the fixed process cycle produces a detectable loss; these contrasts do not establish equivalence. Descriptive rates are reported in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:ablation-results}.

The diagnostic \condSingleWriter\ condition performs substantially worse, but it also changes control granularity and output length. The narrower post-hoc \condSingleContext\ check retains the process loop and shows no detectable pooled difference from \condAgenticCogWriter\ in its exploratory replication (\SingleContextFullRate\ win rate for \condSingleContext, $p=\SingleContextFullP$, unadjusted). Because that run also differs in gateway entry point and transport, it constrains a simple role-context explanation without identifying an alternative mechanism. Appendix~\ref{app:single-context} reports the benchmark-level results and implementation details.

\subsection{Agent behavior (RQ3)}
\label{sec:results-process-dynamics}

The traces show how the available control flexibility is used. The \texttt{Monitor} follows the canonical \texttt{Planning}$\rightarrow$\texttt{Translating}$\rightarrow$\texttt{Reviewing} cycle on \AfourCycleCompliantRate\ of completed runs, returns from \texttt{Reviewing} to \texttt{Planning} on only \AfourReviewingToPlanningRate, and accepts goal regeneration in \AfourRegenerationRuns/\AfourRunCount\ runs. This prevalence contextualizes the \condFixedOrder\ result: unrestricted scheduling often realizes an order close to the imposed cycle. Appendix Table~\ref{tab:process-dynamics} gives the trace summary. Departures from the canonical cycle also do not track a larger pairwise advantage. Against \condFixedOrder, the \condAgenticCogWriter\ win rate is \AfourExactCycleFixedOrderWinRate\ for exact single-cycle traces and \AfourNonExactFixedOrderWinRate\ for other traces; against \condSingleWriter, the corresponding rates are \AfourExactCycleSingleWriterWinRate\ and \AfourNonExactSingleWriterWinRate. These observational splits are descriptive rather than causal. The full sequence visualization, trace table, process counts, and resource use appear in Appendix~\ref{app:process-behavior}.

\subsection{Robustness}
\label{sec:results-robustness}

The RQ1 direction survives the tested output-length and compute restrictions where eligible comparisons are available. The same-family judge favors longer outputs overall, but \condAgenticCogWriter\ remains favored over \condSinglePass\ within the pre-specified length-matching bands and over \condStaged\ and \condTaskPlanning\ under the tightest available compute matching. No \condAgenticCogWriter--\condSinglePass\ pairs fall in that compute band. The cross-family judge preserves the pooled direction for the checked contrasts but returns ties frequently and reverses the WritingBench \condAgenticCogWriter--\condSinglePass\ direction. We therefore treat these analyses as descriptive robustness checks; full results appear in Appendix~\ref{app:sensitivity-analyses}.
