\condAgenticCogWriter\ implements the cognitive process theory of writing as an agent system: a top-level \texttt{Monitor} selects planning, drafting, or reviewing from persistent state and delegates each to a role-specific subagent.
Across three long-form writing benchmarks, pre-specified tests prefer our system to single-pass generation in most cases and to fixed stages and task planning in fewer than half, a post-hoc prompt-level summary favors it over all three, ablations keep most of this advantage, and most traces follow a planning--drafting--reviewing path with few returns to planning.
