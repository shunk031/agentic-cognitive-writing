\condAgenticCogWriter\ reframes long-form writing for AI agents as process-level control: a top-level \texttt{Monitor} selects planning, drafting, or reviewing from persistent state and delegates to role-specific subagents.
Across three long-form writing benchmarks, our system is preferred to single-pass generation, fixed writing stages, and adaptive task planning in same-prompt comparisons; ablations without explicit goals or with a fixed process order keep most of the advantage, and most traces follow a simple planning--drafting--reviewing path.
