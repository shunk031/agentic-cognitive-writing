\condAgenticCogWriter\ implements the cognitive process theory of writing as an agent system: a top-level \texttt{Monitor} selects planning, drafting, or reviewing from persistent state and delegates each to a role-specific subagent.
Across three long-form writing benchmarks, our system is preferred to single-pass generation, fixed writing stages, and adaptive task planning in prompt-level comparisons; ablations without explicit goals or with a fixed process order keep most of the advantage over these alternatives, and most traces follow a simple planning--drafting--reviewing path.
