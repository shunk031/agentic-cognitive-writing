\condAgenticCogWriter\ is a writing skill, motivated by cognitive process theory, whose \texttt{Monitor} chooses planning, drafting, or reviewing over a persistent draft.
On three benchmarks, pre-specified tests prefer it to single-pass generation in most cases and to fixed stages and task planning in fewer than half; post-hoc analyses favor it over all three, and removing goals, adaptive ordering, and subagents leaves it at parity.
