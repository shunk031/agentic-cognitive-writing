\condAgenticCogWriter\ implements the cognitive process theory of writing as an agent skill whose \texttt{Monitor} chooses planning, drafting, or reviewing over a persistent draft and delegates each to a subagent.
Pre-specified tests on three benchmarks prefer our system to single-pass generation in most cases and to fixed stages and task planning in fewer than half, a post-hoc prompt-level summary favors it over all three, and ablations keep most of it.
