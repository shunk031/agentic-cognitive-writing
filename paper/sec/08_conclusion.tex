\condAgenticCogWriter\ reframes long-form writing for AI agents as process-level control: a \texttt{Monitor} selects and delegates planning, drafting, or reviewing from persistent state.
Across three benchmarks, pairwise evaluation favors our system over single-pass, fixed-stage, and adaptive task-planning baselines.
Component analyses and process traces support a coordinated, usually simple adaptive loop, making cognitive writing theory an executable, falsifiable system hypothesis.
