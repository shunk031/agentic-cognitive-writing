\condAgenticCogWriter\ reframes long-form writing as process-level control: a top-level \texttt{Monitor} selects planning, drafting, or reviewing from persistent state and delegates the selected process.
Across three benchmarks, pairwise evaluation favors our system over single-pass generation, fixed writing stages, and adaptive task planning.
Component analyses and process traces support a coordinated writing loop whose ordering is adaptive but usually simple.
These results make cognitive writing theory an executable, falsifiable design basis for writing agents.
