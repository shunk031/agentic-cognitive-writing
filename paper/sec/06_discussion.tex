Our system's gain does not rest mainly on adaptive process selection: no single ablation in Section~\ref{sec:results-mechanism} removes most of our system's advantage, and selection adds only a small post-hoc advantage over \condFixedOrder, as the \texttt{Monitor} usually ends after one pass and, after a revision request, a fixed cycle also returns to planning (Section~\ref{sec:results-process-dynamics}).
Benchmarks whose correct next process is not always planning, or whose requirements change during drafting, are the next step, and the combined ablation in the Limitations would test whether a shared component carries the gain.
