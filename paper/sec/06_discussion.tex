On these tasks the advantage over the fixed pipeline goes with a \texttt{Monitor}-run pass over a persistent workspace draft with the role skills, not with goals, adaptive ordering, or subagents: \condLoopOnly\ removes all three yet matches our system and beats \condStaged, unlike \condStagedRoles, though the \texttt{Monitor}, workspace files, in-place editing, and role guidance are not separated.
Adaptive selection, at most a small post-hoc gain alone, is seldom exercised, and after a revision request a fixed cycle also replans (Section~\ref{sec:results-process-dynamics}), so benchmarks that require it are the next step.
