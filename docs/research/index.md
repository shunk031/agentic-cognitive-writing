# Research

Agentic CogWriter connects a cognitive account of writing with contemporary agent architectures for long-form language-model generation.

The research documentation records the evidence used to make the system and evaluation design decisions in this repository.

## Research foundations

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-account-cog: **Cognitive process theory**

    ---

    The system is organized around the recursive interaction of `Planning`, `Translating`, and `Reviewing`, coordinated by a `Monitor`, with writing guided by an evolving hierarchy of goals.

    [Cognitive process architecture →](../theory-mapping.md)

-   :material-robot-outline: **Skills and subagents**

    ---

    The platform survey examines how contemporary coding agents expose reusable skills, subagents, orchestration, context, and persistent state.

    [Skills & subagents survey →](skill-subagent-survey.md)

-   :material-text-box-check-outline: **Writing evaluation**

    ---

    The evaluation survey examines benchmarks, human anchors, automated judges, long-form writing criteria, licensing, and reproducibility.

    [Writing evaluation & datasets →](writing-eval-datasets.md)

</div>

## From research to experiment design

The research documents are not independent literature notes. They provide the evidence used to define the experimental conditions, benchmark selection, evaluation procedure, and process-analysis methodology for Agentic CogWriter.
